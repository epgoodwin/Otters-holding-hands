01  /  Logo

The
Mark

The GRVTY wordmark is the foundation of our visual identity. Bold, compressed, and unapologetic. It carries the weight of our mission — to deliver decisive technology at the edge of conflict.

Never stretch, skew, or alter the wordmark proportions.
Maintain clear space equal to the height of the "G" on all sides.
Use only approved color combinations: white on black, black on white, or Centurion Crimson on black.
Do not place the wordmark over busy photography without sufficient contrast.
Minimum reproduction size: 80px wide (digital), 20mm (print).

04  /  Photography

The
Lens

GRVTY imagery is direct and uncompromising. We reference the visual language of photojournalism: high contrast, purposeful framing, human presence within systems of precision. Every image must earn its place.

01 High contrast. Shots favor dramatic light and shadow. Avoid flat, evenly lit corporate imagery.
02 Human scale. Show people in context — operators, engineers, decision-makers — as agents of action, not props.
03 Purposeful framing. Asymmetric compositions. Room to breathe on one side. Edge-to-edge tension.
04 Desaturated palette. Process all imagery through the GRVTY preset: reduce saturation, crush blacks, lift grain.
05 Never stock-photo aesthetics. No forced smiles, no generic office environments, no clipart-adjacent tech imagery.

05  /  Visual Elements

The
System

Graphic elements reinforce GRVTY's structural aesthetic. They are drawn from technical drawing conventions — measurement marks, grid systems, registration corners — adapted for brand expression.

Slash Rule
Used as a separator or accent. Angled at 20° to suggest forward motion and urgency.
Grid Lines
Technical grid references backgrounds and data visualizations. Never purely decorative.
Registration Corner
L-shaped frame elements drawn from technical drafting. Used to frame imagery and callouts.
Data Bars
Vertical bar system for data expression. Crimson highlights peak values. Gray for baseline.
Hatch Texture
Fine diagonal hatch pattern, referencing engineering blueprints. Used sparingly as fill.
Crimson Rule
Horizontal rule with a crimson lead. Divides content sections in editorial layouts.

07  /  Tone of Voice

The
Voice

GRVTY communicates with the directness of operational language. We are not a brand that hedges. We do not use jargon to obscure — we use precision language to illuminate.

Direct
Blunt or dismissive
Confident
Arrogant or boastful
Technical
Jargon-heavy or opaque
Urgent
Alarmist or reactive
Mission-Driven
Ideological or political
Human
Casual or flippant
Do — Mission Brief
"GRVTY's sensor fusion platform reduces decision latency from minutes to seconds. Deployed across contested airspace in three operational theaters. Built for the environments where legacy systems fail."
Don't — Corporate Filler
"We are excited to announce our industry-leading, best-in-class innovative defense solutions that leverage synergies across our portfolio to deliver value to our stakeholder ecosystem."
Do — Product Launch
"Field-hardened. Adversary-aware. Ready before the order is given. ARCHER v2 ships Q1. Answer the challenge."
Don't — Over-qualified
"Our solution may potentially offer some advantages in certain operational scenarios depending on mission parameters and customer requirements."
Do — About Us
"We are engineers, operators, and analysts who have stood where the threat is real. We build what we wish we'd had."
